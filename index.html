Impact-Based Flood Forecast Viewer

Flash flood and urban flood early warning, IBF component. AHWA Lab, University of Iowa. WMO project, 2026.

These operational maps combine flood likelihood from ensemble flood-map libraries with potential impact severity for every building, road segment and district. The warning colour of each feature comes from the Flood Risk Matrix of the Scottish Flood Forecasting Service and the UK Flood Guidance Statement, as applied operationally in Speight et al. (2018). Green means business as usual. Yellow means be aware. Amber means be prepared. Red means take action, with danger to life.
